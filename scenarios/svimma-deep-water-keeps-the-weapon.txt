#! nostage
#
# Svimma: in deep water a weapon stays in hand, can be taken out, costs more to swing, and the
# things the player did not allow are still put away.
#
# Written against the code, never run. Nothing in it is verified in game yet.
#
# Six claims, in this order:
#
#   0. You are swimming with no ground under you. The scenario cannot put you there, so it waits up
#      to 90 seconds for you to swim out, and fails saying so if you never do. Every claim after
#      this one is about deep water and means nothing on a beach.
#   1. A sword equips while swimming. Vanilla's EquipItem refuses it, so "equip=ok" is the first
#      thing only Svimma can say.
#   2. Two seconds later the sword is still in the right hand. Vanilla's UpdateEquipment hides the
#      hands every frame while you swim, so a sword that survived a wait is the second.
#   3. A swing starts, and what it was charged is 1.50 times what vanilla charges. The ratio is the
#      one number that does not depend on the sword, the skill or the food eaten, because both halves
#      are read from the same call.
#   4. A pickaxe equips too.
#   5. A bow, a hammer and a torch are refused (AllowBows and AllowBuildingTools are off by default,
#      and a torch is never allowed), and so is a chest piece: the refusal is vanilla's, kept for
#      everything that is not a hand item.
#
# NEEDS: singleplayer, stamina full, Svimma's defaults in the cfg (DeepSwingStaminaMultiplier 1.5,
# AllowBows and AllowBuildingTools off, AllowMelee and AllowPickaxes on) and Enabled true. A host with
# other values changes the answers, which is the rule working. Start the scenario standing on the
# shore, then swim out until the step after the first one passes. Devkit for `give`, `clear`, `mod`,
# `until` and `printed`.
#
# LEAVES: nothing in the world. Devkit empties the pack and hands back the test kit.
#
# MANUAL STEPS, for what no step can see.
#
#   1. Swim out, take the sword out, and press the attack button. Expected: the swing plays and does
#      not look broken (no sliding, no frozen pose), and you stay afloat. If the animation is wrong
#      the code is right and the animator state for swimming is what has to be dealt with.
#   2. Swing at a creature or a tree standing in or just beyond the water. Expected: it takes damage.
#      Hits on things under the surface are the unknown the ticket named.
#   3. In deep water, press the hide key twice. Expected: the sword goes away, then comes out again.
#      Vanilla would leave it hidden until you stood on the ground.
#   4. Hold the block button with a shield in the other hand. Expected: nothing, no block pose, and
#      `svimma` reports blocking=no. Set AllowBlocking true: it blocks.
#   5. With the fishing rod in hand, cast from deep water. Expected: the float lands and you can bite
#      and reel.
#   6. Wade out until the water is at your chest but your feet are on the bottom. Expected: swings cost
#      the ordinary price (`svimma cost` reports none) and nothing in this mod is involved.
#   7. Swim for a while, swinging, until the bar is nearly empty. Expected: a swing is refused when you
#      cannot pay it, and an empty bar while swimming drowns you exactly as in vanilla.
#   8. On a server with the host's DeepSwingStaminaMultiplier set to 2 and yours to 1.5: swings charge 2.
#      Verbose in your own cfg still logs, because it is the one setting the host never sets.


# ---- 0: deep water

mod svimma
until 90 deep=yes
printed enabled=yes yes

clear
give SwordBronze:1,PickaxeBronze:1,Bow:1,Hammer:1,Torch:1,ArmorLeatherChest:1


# ---- 1 and 2: the sword equips and stays

mod svimma equip SwordBronze
printed equip=ok yes
wait 2
mod svimma
printed right=SwordBronze yes
printed deep=yes yes


# ---- 3: a swing, and its price

mod svimma swing
printed swing=started yes
wait 3
mod svimma cost
printed ratio=1.50 yes


# ---- 4: the pickaxe

mod svimma equip PickaxeBronze
printed equip=ok yes


# ---- 5: what stays put away

mod svimma equip Bow
printed equip=refused yes

mod svimma equip Hammer
printed equip=refused yes

mod svimma equip Torch
printed equip=refused yes

mod svimma equip ArmorLeatherChest
printed equip=refused yes
